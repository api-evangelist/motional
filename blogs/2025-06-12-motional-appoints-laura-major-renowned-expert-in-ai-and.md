Today, Motional announced that Laura Major has been confirmed as its President and Chief Executive Officer (CEO). Major was appointed by Motional’s Board of Directors following a successful tenure as Chief Technology Officer (CTO). The announcement comes as Motional accelerates its AI development and prepares to launch its driverless commercial service next year.
